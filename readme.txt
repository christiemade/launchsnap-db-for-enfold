Save All Form Entries from Enfold's Contact Form Module into your Wordpress database

== Description == 

Save the information filled out by users on your websites forms build with Enfold's Contact Forms and/or Contact Form 7.  They will appear by Form in a Form Entries tab in the Dashboard.
